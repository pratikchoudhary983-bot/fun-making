About

Crafting moments that feel like memories before you've finished living them.

'26

For the last six years I've been documenting a slower, softer way of living — from sunrise hikes in the Algarve to candle-lit dinners in Kyoto. My work sits at the intersection of cinematic storytelling and honest, unfiltered presence.

I partner with brands who care about craft, sustainability, and the people on the other side of the screen. Every campaign is treated like a small film — concepted, shot, edited and scored by me and a tiny team I trust.
